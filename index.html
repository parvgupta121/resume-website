Project 01 · TAPMI

Portfolio Risk Optimisation

Business Mathematics · Quantitative finance

Developed an Indian equity portfolio risk analysis and optimisation solution using historical stock returns and computational methods. The project explored how covariance matrices and matrix algebra can help measure and minimise portfolio risk.

Methods & implementation

  • Worked with historical prices and monthly returns for Indian listed equities.
  • Calculated covariance matrices and portfolio variance using WᵀΣW.
  • Compared an equal-weight benchmark with a minimum-variance portfolio.
  • Developed Python-based analysis and an interactive Streamlit application.
Python Pandas NumPy SciPy Streamlit Plotly Excel
Key application: Translating business mathematics into a practical framework for understanding investment risk and portfolio trade-offs.

Project 03 · AI & Actuarial Workflows

AI-powered actuarial workflow prototype

  • Explored the role of AI-assisted insight generation and reporting.

Project 05 · Accounting

Polycab India – Complete Accounting Cycle

Financial accounting · Annual report analysis

A practical accounting project based on Polycab India Limited's audited FY 2024–25 standalone financial statements. The project involved analysing the annual report, establishing opening balances, recording transactions and completing the accounting cycle through financial statement preparation.

Accounting work

  • Analysed the company's audited Balance Sheet and relevant annual report notes.
  • Established opening balances and prepared journal entries.
  • Recorded transactions and prepared ledger accounts and trial balances.
  • Worked on year-end adjustments, including depreciation, provisions and expected credit losses.
  • Prepared the Trading Account, Profit & Loss Account and Balance Sheet.
Financial Accounting Journal Entries Ledger Accounts Trial Balance Annual Report Analysis Financial Statements
Key application: Applying accounting principles to a real company's published financial information and a structured accounting-cycle exercise.
Project 06 · Power BI

ChaiNest Cafés – Sales Analytics Dashboard

Business intelligence · Data visualisation

A Power BI project analysing the 2025 sales performance of a fictional café chain operating across 10 Indian cities. The project involved cleaning and transforming sales data, combining datasets, analysing product and customer behaviour, and evaluating store performance against annual targets.

Dashboard & analysis

  • Cleaned and transformed sales data using Power Query.
  • Appended monthly sales datasets and merged store and product information.
  • Created calculated columns to support business analysis.
  • Analysed revenue trends, product performance, customer behaviour and order patterns.
  • Built three report pages: Sales Overview, Products & Customers, and Store Targets.
  • Compared actual store revenue against annual targets.
Power BI Power Query Data Cleaning Data Transformation Data Visualisation Business Intelligence
Key application: Turning raw sales data into interactive dashboards that help evaluate business performance and support decision-making.
