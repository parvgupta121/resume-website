Project 01 · TAPMI

Portfolio Risk Optimisation

Business Mathematics · Quantitative finance

Developed an Indian equity portfolio risk analysis and optimisation solution using historical stock returns and computational methods. The project explored how covariance matrices and matrix algebra can help measure and minimise portfolio risk.

Methods & implementation

  • Worked with historical prices and monthly returns for Indian listed equities.
  • Calculated covariance matrices and portfolio variance using WᵀΣW.
  • Compared an equal-weight benchmark with a minimum-variance portfolio.
  • Developed Python-based analysis and an interactive Streamlit application.
PythonPandasNumPySciPyStreamlitPlotlyExcel
Key application: Translating business mathematics into a practical framework for understanding investment risk and portfolio trade-offs.
Project 02 · TAPMI

AI and Food

Anthropology / DIR · Technology in food services

An interdisciplinary study of how digital technologies shape food-service operations and customer experiences. The project used two case studies to observe technology in everyday institutional and restaurant settings.

Case studies

  • Blue Dove Mess, MAHE Bengaluru: studied biometric identification for student recognition and meal-subscription access management.
  • Nasi and Mee, Galleria Mall, Bengaluru: examined the use of accounting software and digital customer loyalty/reward systems in restaurant operations and engagement.
AnthropologyAI & digital technologyFood-service operationsCustomer experienceCase study
Key application: Documenting how digital systems can support access management, operational processes and customer engagement.

Project 05 · TAPMI

Polycab India – Complete Accounting Cycle

Financial accounting · Annual report analysis

A practical accounting project based on Polycab India Limited’s audited FY 2024–25 standalone financial statements. The project involved analysing the annual report, establishing opening balances, recording transactions and completing the accounting cycle through financial statement preparation. FY 2025–26 transactions and closing statements are simulated, not actual Polycab financial results.

Accounting work

  • Analysed the company’s audited Balance Sheet and relevant annual report notes.
  • Established opening balances and prepared journal entries, ledger accounts and trial balances.
  • Worked on year-end adjustments, including depreciation, provisions and expected credit losses.
  • Prepared the Trading Account, Profit & Loss Account and Balance Sheet.
Financial AccountingAnnual Report AnalysisJournal EntriesLedgersTrial BalanceFinancial Statements
Key application: Translating published financial statement information into a structured accounting model and completing the accounting cycle.
Project 06 · Power BI

ChaiNest Cafés – Sales Analytics Dashboard

Business intelligence · Data visualisation

A Power BI project analysing the 2025 sales performance of a fictional café chain operating across 10 Indian cities. The project involved cleaning and transforming sales data, combining datasets, analysing product and customer behaviour, and evaluating store performance against annual targets.

Analysis & dashboard

  • Cleaned and transformed sales data using Power Query; appended monthly sales datasets and merged store and product information.
  • Created calculated columns to support business analysis.
  • Analysed revenue trends, product performance, customer behaviour and order patterns.
  • Built three report pages: Sales Overview, Products & Customers, and Store Targets.
  • Compared actual store revenue against annual targets.
  • Project dataset: 600 sales rows, 10 stores, ₹4,73,725 revenue and 600 orders; 5 of 10 stores met their targets, with overall revenue at 98% of target.
Power BIPower QueryExcelData CleaningData TransformationData VisualisationBusiness Intelligence
Key application: Turning raw sales data into interactive dashboards that help evaluate business performance and support decision-making.
